Humanitarian Funding: Need vs. Delivery
WFP annual funding requirements vs. received · billions USD · 2018–2024
From 2018–2022, WFP funding kept pace with rising needs. In 2023, requirements reached $22.8B but received only $8.6B — a 62% funding gap. In 2024, WFP received just 41 cents of every dollar needed. The $14B+ shortfall drives suspended operations and reduced rations for tens of millions. Source: WFP Annual Performance Reports.
